What I need you to decide#

1. Which price to store for a Venice call#

Venice does not send back what a picture or a clip cost. For a video, it answers a price question before the call ("this clip will cost $0.42"). For a picture, it publishes a price per model in its model list. The exact amount charged appears only in Venice's billing history, and reading that needs a stronger "admin" Venice key.

OptionWhat happensWhat it costs
A — Venice's own stated priceVideo: the price Venice quotes just before the call. Picture: the model's listed price for the chosen size, read at call time. Stored with the run and shown as "Venice price".Small. May differ by cents from the bill if Venice changes prices that day.
B — A, then the billed amountAs A. A nightly job then replaces each price with the billed amount from Venice's billing history.Needs an admin Venice key stored as well, plus one more background job and its tests.
C — Balance differenceRead the account balance before and after each call.Wrong as soon as two calls run at once. Not recommended.

My recommendation: A — it is the price Venice itself states for that call, and it needs no second key.
